What the twin assumes

The plank is a cantilever fixed at the clamp with a point load at the tip. For that case the tip deflection is two-thirds of the overhang times the tip rotation in radians. The limit is the overhang divided by the ratio set in the code, which is a demonstration value, not a design criterion.
